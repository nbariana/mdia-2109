- *Project Description*
  
  **Seventh Hell** is a turn-based pixel RPG set in a school during a zombie outbreak. Players control Kim, who must fight through six (7) floors to escape the building. Combat requires managing limited resources and choosing the right weapons for different enemy types.
  
The game features two difficulty modes: Regular restarts you at the current floor when you die, while Hard Mode sends you back to the beginning. Players can explore for better loot, and unlock shortcuts. Enemies get tougher as you descend deeper and get closer to Judson, the final boss.
